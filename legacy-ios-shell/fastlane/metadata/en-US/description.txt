Foundation verifies you are a real human and that your government-issued ID is authentic — without ever uploading your face, your ID photo, your MRZ, or your device fingerprint.

How it works:
• Tap your passport or national ID against your phone's NFC chip. A zero-knowledge proof verifies the issuing state's signature without revealing your name, document number, or photo.
• Capture a short liveness video. A device-only face-match model confirms the chip photo and your selfie are the same person.
• iOS App Attest + the Secure Enclave sign the result with a biometric-gated key. Only a hash of that signed proof — never your biometrics, never your ID — is anchored on Solana.

What leaves your device:
• A hash of the signed attestation.
• A commitment recorded on Solana.

That's it. No selfie, no ID photo, no document number, no phone fingerprint, no IP-keyed profile. We literally cannot leak what we never collected.

Why this matters: commercial ID-verification vendors retain your photo, your ID, and your device fingerprint for years. Their breach surface is every customer they've ever onboarded. Foundation's breach surface is zero, by construction.

Foundation is Pillar 1 of a broader platform that returns voice, data, and markets to people rather than intermediaries.

Requires an invited email. App Attest requires a real device (iOS 16+).
